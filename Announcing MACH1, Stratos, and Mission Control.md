# Announcing MACH1, Stratos, and Mission Control

Oct 26, 2026 · @Adam

Colleagues,

I'm writing to introduce three projects that will shape how we build, deliver, and work together as one organization: **Project MACH1**, **Project Stratos**, and **Project Mission Control**. Each tackles a different part of the same challenge. Together, they are how we turn the convergence of our former organizations into a single, modern engineering enterprise.

This is a first look, not a finished plan. I want your thinking in it from the start.

## Three projects, one transformation

**Project MACH1: fixing the architecture from the bottom up.** MACH1 moves our portfolio to a MACH architecture built on four principles: Verifiable, Composable, Connected, and Resilient. The target is a layer of capability platforms run as products, a superintelligence (SI) orchestration layer above them, and a small set of SuperApps that unify the user experience. Its pillars are Strengthen the Foundation, Standardize the Platforms, Streamline the Portfolio, and Scale the Intelligence. Through December, we focus on the foundation: bringing every team onto Nitro and Platforma and clearing the hygiene work, such as end-of-life dependencies, that holds us back.

**Project Stratos: fixing the experience from the top down.** Stratos is MACH1's counterpart. Where MACH1 rebuilds what sits underneath, Stratos makes sure what our users touch is coherent, usable, and worth their time. Its pillars are Engage the Workforce, Empower the Users, Evolve the Capabilities, and Enable the Developers. It holds us to five red lines: no user left stranded; nothing ships without the user in the room; no surprises on day one; the human stays in command; and we measure outcomes, not activity.

**Project Mission Control: changing how we work.** Mission Control is about how we plan, deliver, and steer as an agile organization. Its pillars are Align the Work, Accelerate the Flow, Amplify the Teams, and Adapt the Course. It includes an integrated program view that draws on real-time data, so leaders see the truth of our progress and teams spend less time on manual reporting.

## Aligned to the Data Sprint

These projects are not a separate agenda. They are how our organization delivers on the Data Sprint.

- **MACH1** builds the platforms the Data Sprint depends on: composable, connected services with clean interfaces, so data moves across the portfolio instead of staying locked inside individual applications.
- **Stratos** makes sure that data reaches people in a form they can use, through experiences designed with users and for the decisions they actually make.
- **Mission Control** puts data to work on ourselves, replacing manual status reporting with real-time insight into how our work is flowing and where it needs help.

In short, MACH1 makes our data accessible, Stratos makes it usable, and Mission Control makes it actionable. As the Data Sprint sets priorities, these projects will adjust to support them.
